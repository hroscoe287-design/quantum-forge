# BLOODLINE
## Episode 1 — THE AWAKENING
### Production Draft v1.0

**Genre:** Dark supernatural battle-shōnen / science-fiction mystery  
**Target runtime:** 8–12 minutes

## COLD OPEN

### SCENE 1 — THE LAST WARNING

**INT. SECRET LABORATORY — NIGHT**

Red emergency lights pulse through smoke.

Broken glass floats in the air as alarms scream.

A younger-looking version of **DR. INFINITY**, Diamond's father, runs through the laboratory carrying a damaged research drive.

He stops at a sealed chamber.

Inside the chamber is an empty containment pod.

**DR. INFINITY**
*(breathing hard)*
No... it wasn't supposed to wake yet.

A deep, unnatural vibration rolls through the room.

Dr. Infinity looks directly toward the camera.

**DR. INFINITY**
If you're seeing this, Diamond...

The lights flicker.

**DR. INFINITY**
...then I failed to keep them away from you.

A shadow moves behind him.

Dr. Infinity turns.

CUT TO BLACK.

**DR. INFINITY (V.O.)**
The serum was never the experiment.

A violent blue flash.

## SCENE 2 — THE PENDANT

**INT. DIAMOND'S BEDROOM — MORNING**

Diamond jolts awake.

His breathing is heavy.

He grabs the blue diamond pendant around his neck.

It is ice cold.

Diamond studies it.

**DIAMOND**
Dad...

His phone alarm goes off.

He shuts it off.

**DIAMOND**
Great. Another normal day.

The pendant gives a tiny blue pulse.

Diamond freezes.

**DIAMOND**
...Okay. Maybe not.

---

## SCENE 3 — THE SCIENTIST

**INT. COLLEGE LAB — DAY**

Diamond works on a compact energy prototype.

He adjusts a circuit.

The machine activates.

A clean electrical hum fills the room.

Then—

The pendant pulses.

The machine glitches.

Symbols appear across the monitor.

Diamond stares.

One symbol matches a mark from his father's old research notes.

**DIAMOND**
That's impossible.

His classmate approaches.

**CLASSMATE**
You good?

Diamond quickly closes the screen.

**DIAMOND**
Yeah. Just a bad equation.

He looks back.

The symbol is gone.

---

## SCENE 4 — THE MESSAGE

**INT. CAMPUS COMPUTER LAB — EVENING**

Diamond accesses an encrypted archive left behind by his father.

A hidden video file appears.

He opens it.

Dr. Infinity appears on-screen.

**DR. INFINITY**
Diamond, if you've found this recording, listen carefully.

Diamond leans closer.

**DR. INFINITY**
There are people searching for something called the Bloodline.

Diamond's expression changes.

**DR. INFINITY**
Do not trust anyone who tells you they know what happened to me.

The recording distorts.

**DR. INFINITY**
And whatever happens—

The screen goes black.

**DR. INFINITY**
—do not let them make you awaken it.

The entire campus loses power.

Darkness.

Diamond slowly stands.

**DIAMOND**
Awaken what?

A distant scream.

---

## SCENE 5 — THE HUNTER

**EXT. CAMPUS — NIGHT**

Students run through the darkness.

Diamond sees a strange figure moving between buildings.

Its body appears almost human—but distorted by black supernatural energy.

Diamond steps between the creature and a fallen student.

**DIAMOND**
Hey!

The creature turns.

Diamond's confidence disappears for half a second.

**DIAMOND**
...You're definitely not human.

The creature attacks.

Diamond dodges.

FAST.

It tears through a concrete bench.

Diamond studies its movement.

**DIAMOND**
Left shoulder drops before it strikes.

The creature attacks again.

Diamond moves before the strike lands.

**DIAMOND**
Got you.

He counters.

The creature barely moves.

It slams Diamond into a wall.

Blood runs from his forehead.

Diamond struggles to stand.

**DIAMOND**
Okay...

He wipes the blood away.

**DIAMOND**
That was my best move.

The creature charges.

Diamond raises his arms.

The blue pendant cracks.

---

## SCENE 6 — BLOODLINE

Silence.

A low heartbeat.

**BOOM.**

Blue energy erupts from Diamond.

His eyes flash electric blue.

Dust lifts from the ground around him.

Thin blue markings spread across his arms.

The creature stops.

For the first time, it looks afraid.

Diamond looks at his hands.

**DIAMOND**
What...

His breathing becomes controlled.

The world slows.

He can see every movement.

The creature attacks.

Diamond disappears from its path.

He reappears beside it.

**DIAMOND**
I can see you.

He drives a blue-energy strike into the creature.

**BOOM.**

The creature crashes through the pavement.

Diamond stares at his glowing fist.

Then his knees buckle.

**DIAMOND**
Whatever this is...

He looks at the broken pendant.

**DIAMOND**
...my father knew.

---

## SCENE 7 — THE WATCHERS

**INT. UNKNOWN LOCATION**

Several figures stand around a massive circular display.

A blue pulse appears.

One figure slowly looks up.

**UNKNOWN FIGURE**
The Bloodline has awakened.

Another figure smiles.

**SECOND FIGURE**
Then Diamond Infinity is alive.

A screen displays Diamond's image.

**UNKNOWN FIGURE**
Prepare the hunters.

CUT TO BLACK.
